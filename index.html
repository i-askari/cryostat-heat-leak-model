What this model is

A one-dimensional, steady-state, radial thermal-resistance network for a cylindrical vacuum-insulated cryogenic HTS cable. It is a screening tool for exploring how vacuum quality, insulation and geometry trade against one another, not a cryostat design solver.

Not included

  • Axial conduction, end effects, supports, ports, seals and penetrations
  • Joints and ohmic heating
  • Transient behaviour and coolant fluid dynamics
  • Detailed MLI spacer contacts, compression, seams and installation quality
  • Temperature-dependent wall conductivity, emissivity and convection
  • Radiation across the inner gas gap and the copper former's temperature profile

Validation

Analytical tests and an independent heat-balance solver check synthetic cases. These are mathematical and numerical checks, not experimental validation. See docs/VALIDATION.md.
